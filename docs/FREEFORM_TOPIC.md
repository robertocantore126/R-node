# Freeform topic

Freeform is merged into `main`. AVIF and clipboard fixes: `fix/freeform-clipboard-avif`.

Right-click a topic and choose **New freeform topic**, immediately below **New code topic**. This creates a connected 720 x 480 canvas with a grid.

- Double-click an empty part of the canvas to open a draw.io-style picker: plain text, or a base shape (rectangle, rounded, capsule, circle, ellipse, diamond, hexagon, and the flowchart set: data/parallelogram, process, document, database/cylinder, triangle, note), then any shape saved in the library. Text appears with its top-left at the point; a shape is centred on it at a starting size, outlined in its branch colour, and opens the editor so you can type its label. Escape or a click elsewhere closes the picker. The context menu also offers **Add text / node**. Use the usual Inspector to give an item a box/shape, colour and text formatting.
- Select several items draw.io style: drag on an empty part of the canvas (below its title band) to draw a box; every item lying wholly inside it is selected (Shift keeps the current selection and adds to it). Dragging any selected item then moves the whole selection, as one undo step; it stops at the left margin and under the title band, and pushing it past the right or bottom edge grows the canvas. A click without a drag on a selected item narrows the selection to it. A plain click on the empty canvas still selects the canvas, and its title band is what drags the canvas itself.
- **Diagram from AI** (right-click any topic, or the ✦ button in a canvas's Inspector): describe the diagram or paste as much material as you like, pick a kind (flowchart, architecture, process with swimlanes, concept map, hierarchy, timeline, comparison, or let the AI choose) and **Copy prompt**; paste it into any AI chat; paste the whole answer back into the window and **Import** (or Ctrl+V on the canvas). No API key. The prompt (src/core/diagramPrompt.ts) asks for draw.io XML restricted to what the importer draws — the shapes, swimlane containers with relative children, the quick-style colours, arrow heads and dashes, a 10 px grid with no overlaps — and `tests/diagramPrompt.test.ts` imports its own example and checks every taught style maps to the promised shape. On a canvas the diagram goes below its content; on a topic, into a new canvas under it. **Show prompt** displays it for copying by hand when the clipboard is blocked.
- Paste a draw.io diagram (Ctrl+V): the XML an AI writes when asked for a draw.io diagram (prose and a ```xml fence around it are fine), or a copy out of draw.io itself, plain or compressed. Every box becomes an item with its size, label, fill/outline/text colours, dashes, bold/italic and font size; every edge between two boxes becomes an arrow with its label, colour, width, dashes, heads, pinned ends and straight/elbow/curved style. draw.io's basic and flowchart-stencil names map onto R-node's outlines (decision → diamond, database/cylinder3 → database, data → parallelogram, terminator → capsule, predefined process → process...); shapes R-node does not draw yet (actor, swimlane headers, cloud icons) fall back to a rectangle; edges with a loose end are skipped and the toast says how many. It is one undo step, lands at the canvas's top-left (or below what the canvas already holds), grows the canvas to fit, and stays selected so a drag moves it all. Pasted on an ordinary topic, it creates a new canvas under that topic. Arrows between two items of a canvas are painted over the boxes (on screen and in PNG/SVG/PDF export), so a filled container does not hide them.
- Containers (swimlanes): the picker's last button, or a draw.io swimlane / `container=1` box / any box holding others. A container is a nested canvas drawn as a diagram box: no grid, the title centred in a 36 px band (a swimlane's `fillColor` paints that band, `swimlaneFillColor` the body), square or rounded corners, dashed if draw.io said so. Items inside live in it: they move with it, a marquee started in it picks only its items, its title band drags it, and it grows to hold them. Unlike a canvas it may be small and its contents may touch its edges (no 16 px margin), so draw.io lanes stay flush in their pool. On import, children that would sit under the 36 px band (draw.io headers are usually 23-40 px) move down and their container grows by the same amount; invisible draw.io groups only position their children.
- Colours (Inspector, any topic, applied to the whole selection in one undo step): draw.io's quick styles (blue, green, orange, yellow, red, purple, grey, white, dark — fill, outline and dark text together; on a container they paint the header band) and a clear button; Fill with a colour picker, **None** (see-through) and **Auto** (the theme's branch colour), plus a fill-opacity slider stored as rgba(); Text colour; the whole item's Opacity (also applied in SVG/PDF export); a container's Header colour. Arrows get a Colour picker, Auto, and the same palette's outline colours. Imported solid draw.io arrows are now solid (an R-node arrow with no line style is dashed).
- Drag existing topics into the canvas. Their subtrees and relative geometry are preserved. Drag the items inside to reposition them.
- Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copies selected images, side-image slots, gallery cells, individual topics or multiple selected branches. Pasted items remain separate items in their canvas, offset from the source; rich text, sizes, internal links and image references are preserved. Browser clipboard restrictions have a session fallback, and native paste events handle images copied as files.
- Drop plain text, image files or browser images into the canvas. AVIF is accepted alongside PNG/JPEG/GIF/WebP, including typeless `.avif` files. Images become independent movable items. Existing image attachments can also be dragged into it.
- Paste text or images with the canvas selected. Shape-library drops are positioned at the pointer.
- Ordinary topics inside a freeform canvas have a transparent fill by default. Picking Fill in the Inspector or a context-menu colour gives text-only items a visible box; resetting the colour restores transparency. Explicitly chosen fills are preserved.
- Draw arrows draw.io style: select an item and drag one of the four blue arrows around it onto another topic. Dropping near the target's border pins the arrow to that point (snapping to the quarter marks); dropping in its middle leaves that end floating. New arrows are solid, straight, with a head at the target. The Link… command still works too.
- Select an arrow to edit it: drag either round end handle onto another topic (or another side of the same one) to reconnect it. The Inspector sets the line (curved / straight / elbow), dash style, width, the head at each end (none, triangle, open, diamond, circle), reverses it, or unpins its ends. Elbow lines are routed orthogonally but do not avoid other boxes.
- Notes: right-click the canvas and choose **Add note**. The note is a floating topic beside the canvas, like a chart box's note, with subtopics and images. It appears only through **Show notes** in the same menu, not by clicking the canvas, and a corner mark says a canvas has one. Handwriting is not read as text, so the note is what a quiz or deck generated from the canvas sees.
- Write by hand: use the pen button at the top left of the map (the eraser is under it), press **P** with the canvas selected, or use **Write with pen** in its right-click menu or the Inspector. Strokes have a constant thickness, set in pixels (at 100% zoom) with the slider beside the pen button, and draw over the items on the canvas; a mouse writes too. The Inspector also sets the colour, switches to the eraser and clears the canvas. A Wacom eraser end erases whole strokes at any time, without entering pen mode. Each stroke is one undo step, Escape abandons the one in progress, and writing past the edge grows the canvas when the pen lifts. The title band still drags the canvas in pen mode. P or Escape stops writing. The right-click menu is off while the pen or eraser is active; right-drag still pans. The eraser end needs **Use Windows Ink** enabled in the Wacom driver settings; without it the browser sees the pen as a mouse, which still writes.
- Move the outer canvas to move its contents together. Resize using its lower-right handle or the Inspector's canvas width/height fields. Shrinking stops at the occupied content bounds. Adding or enlarging content grows the affected canvas and its enclosing canvases in the same undo step.
- Moving a topic already inside a canvas preserves its parent and connector. Left-edge resizing preserves the opposite edge and stops at the canvas margin.
- Deleting a selected image removes its image reference; its topic, notes, quizzes and other data remain available. Delete the topic explicitly when you want to remove all of it.
- Changes use the existing document operations and undo/redo. Local coordinates are serialized with the document. Canvas contents remain fixed through automatic layout and global reflow.

This is a native freeform diagram surface, not an embedded diagrams.net application. R-node's existing shape and relationship tools apply; draw.io file import and its complete toolbox are not included.

## Validation

Automated coverage includes the original freeform tests plus `tests/freeformHardening.test.ts`: the five pre-merge defects now have regressions requiring correct behavior, with additional coverage for nested canvas growth, subtree capture with negative offsets, and containment during resize. `tests/rnodeFile.test.ts` also writes and rereads an actual SQLite `.rnode` container, preserving local geometry, relationships, notes and image bytes.

Latest automated checks: `npm test` passed all 611 tests across 36 files, `npm run typecheck` passed, `npm run build` passed, `npm run check:map` passed, and `git diff --check` passed. Clipboard tests use simulated clipboard APIs, and the AVIF worker test uses a simulated decoder; actual OS clipboard behavior and AVIF decoding still require browser/Tauri verification. The hit-test regression uses a simulated Canvas2D context and does not establish pixel fidelity or real pointer behavior.

Browser UI smoke and the real-browser parity harness are **not verified** in this session: the browser automation inventory returned no available browsers. Desktop/Tauri runtime is also untested. The PDF command exports vector text, freeform surfaces and grids; see [PDF_PRINT.md](PDF_PRINT.md) for format tradeoffs and validation.
